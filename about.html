About Pax Silica

Who we are, what we believe in, and how our team came together.

Who We Are

Peace through silicon

Pax Silica is a group of three Bachelor of Science in Information Technology students who share one goal: to build software that is useful, clean and dependable.

The name blends the Latin word pax, meaning peace, with silica, the material behind every chip. To us it means calm, well-crafted technology that makes work easier for people.

We work across web and desktop development, and we learn by building real systems for real users.

Our Journey

A short look at how Pax Silica has grown.

Step 01

Learning the Fundamentals

We started with HTML, CSS, PHP and MySQL, building small projects to master the basics.

Step 02

Building Real Systems

We moved on to school portals, student information systems and store tools used by real people.

Step 03

Going Desktop

We explored VB.NET to bring the same ideas to Windows desktop applications.

Step 04

Forming Pax Silica

We came together as one team, with one identity, to share knowledge and grow our portfolio.
